HOW IT WORKS

Your keys stay yours.

Candylario makes an agent wallet for your account and keeps its key sealed. Your own wallet approves it with a signature: from then on it can open and close positions for you, with their stops, and it can never withdraw.

This is Hyperliquid's testnet: you need a testnet account there. Hyperliquid's faucet gives one test funds once its address has made a deposit on mainnet.
